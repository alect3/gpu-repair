# Board A

- Model: Zotac RTX 3090 Trinity
- Serial: 0089973886
- Status: same U15 fault as Board B. 5V short cleared 2026-09-09. The two
  1.5k resistors are still on the U15 pads; lift them, fit the 0R link, boot
  test. See the README for the fault chain.

## Symptoms

- No power at all when booted. No fans, no lights, no POST.
- The PC itself ran fine with the card installed. The PSU did not trip, so
  the card is not shorting under power. It is simply not enabling.

## Findings

- Resistance to ground is normal on every rail: 12V, 5V, 3.3V, 1.8V, PEX,
  core and memory
  (measured 2026-09-05).
- Two capacitors and a resistor array had fallen off (same as Board B, see
  [common.md](../common.md)). Re-soldered before testing began. 5V reads
  normal to ground, so the re-fit on this board did not introduce a short.
- 12V input path from the 8-pin connectors to the VRM input is intact
  (continuity, 2026-09-05).
- The 5V regulator on the back, directly behind the re-fitted parts, has
  its output at about 30 mV when powered and the IC warms slowly over a
  long run. Same as Board B. Hard short on the 5V net, regulator sitting
  in current limit.
- Working theory now: same root cause as Board B. The impact that knocked
  the parts off damaged the 5V regulator area on both boards. The earlier
  "5V resistance normal" reading needs re-checking at this inductor.

### 2026-09-05

- Two capacitors and a resistor array had fallen off (same as Board B). Re-soldered
  them before testing.
- 12V rail resistance to ground is fine. No short on 12V input.
- 1.8V rail resistance to ground is fine.
- PEX rail resistance to ground is fine.
- Core and memory rail resistance to ground seem normal.
- 5V rail resistance to ground is fine.
- 3.3V rail resistance to ground is fine.
- Looked for input fuses. None found.
- Continuity from 8-pin 12V pins to VRM input is fine.
- Slot 12V fingers to the core VRM high-side FET drains: no continuity
  beep, about 50 kohm in resistance mode. Not yet known whether this is a
  fault or by design (see next steps).
- First powered test. 12V present on both 8-pins. 0V on 5V, 1.8V, PEX,
  core and memory. 3.3V not measured. Card was on a riser fed from the
  ATX PSU, riser light on, no PC. Slot 12V and 3.3V delivery not yet
  verified at the riser.
- Confirmed 3.3V at the riser's regulator output. Slot power is being
  delivered. The dead small rails are a real fault on the card.
- Small regulator inductors, powered on riser, viewed from the front:
  - Top left inductor: 0V. Regulator IC beside it is marked GS9216 (see
    [notes/gs9216.md](../notes/gs9216.md)). Rail identity not yet
    confirmed; 3090 boards use this chip for 1.8V and for PEX.
  - Bottom left inductor, assumed 1.8V: about 300 mV. Not a dead rail,
    something is either leaking into it or the regulator is trying and
    failing to start.
- Bottom right (front) inductor sits where the re-fitted caps and resistor
  are. On a similar layout (Inno3D 3090, GPU Solutions video, see
  [common.md](../common.md)) that inductor is the 5V rail. The regulator IC
  for it is on the back of the board and gets warm when powered, with the
  rail at 0V. Suspect the 5V circuit is faulty on this board too, not just
  Board B: A fails dead with a hot regulator, B fails short.
- Both re-fitted capacitors read 0 ohm across them, unpowered. Same on
  Board B. So the 5V circuit is shorted on this board too, matching the
  warm regulator IC.
- The two discrete 1.5 kohm resistors (replacing the array) read 1.5 kohm
  and 1.0 kohm in place. The 1.0 kohm is a parallel path, normal. Board B
  reads 1.5 kohm and 0 ohm at the same positions.
- Re-measured the 5V inductor: about 30 mV, not 0V. Same as Board B. The
  regulator IC only warmed up after a long run; it does not heat quickly.
  So the temperature difference from Board B was run time, not a
  different fault.

### 2026-09-09

  caps and the two discrete 1.5 kohm resistors). 5V to ground now reads
  normal, unpowered. Not yet powered. Root cause of the short not yet
  recorded: was it a bridge, a cracked cap, or placement?
- Core rail resistance to ground: 1 to 5 ohm. Same on both boards. Normal

## Next steps

5V short cleared by rework (2026-09-09). Now powered, in order:

1. 5V at the bottom right inductor. Expect 5V. Watch the regulator IC on
   the back for heat.
2. 1.8V at the bottom left inductor (was 300 mV of leakage).
3. Top left GS9216 inductor (was 0V). If it comes up, note the voltage so
   the rail is identified.
4. 3.3V on the card, PEX, then core and memory at their chokes.
5. If 5V is up and a later rail is still dead, that rail's regulator is the
   next target. See [notes/gs9216.md](../notes/gs9216.md) for the top left
   one.
6. If everything comes up, fans should spin. Cut power within 20 seconds
   with no cooling on the die, then sort out a heatsink before a longer run
   or a PC boot.
