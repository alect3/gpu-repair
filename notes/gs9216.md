# GS9216 buck regulator

GStek (NIKO-SEM) single synchronous buck, TQFN23 4x4 mm. Used on the small
rails of RTX 3090 boards. Repair threads report it on both the 1.8V rail and
the PEX rail on various 3090 designs, so which rail a given one serves has to
be confirmed by measurement.

Board A has one marked GS9216 at the top left (front). Output 0V.

## Datasheet

The GS9216 datasheet is not freely available. The GS9238 from the same
family and package is: [datasheets/GS9238-family-TQFN23.pdf](datasheets/GS9238-family-TQFN23.pdf).
The pinout below is from that document. Treat it as very likely but not
certain to match the GS9216 pin for pin. Sanity check by confirming pin 7
(AIN) reads 12V and pin 21 (VCC) reads about 5V before trusting the rest.

## Pinout (TQFN23, pin 1 at the dot, counting counterclockwise)

| Pin | Name  | What it is                                                  |
|-----|-------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1   | PGOOD | Open-drain power good output                                |
| 2   | EN    | Enable. High = run, low = shutdown. Rising threshold 1.6V   |
| 3   | PFM   | Mode select. Tied high = forced PWM, low = PFM              |
| 4   | AGND  | Analog ground                                               |
| 5   | FB    | Feedback, from a divider between output and AGND            |
| 6   | TON   | On-time set, resistor from VIN to TON                       |
| 7   | AIN   | Supply for the internal analog circuitry. Needs 6V or more  |
| 8, 9, 22, 24 | VIN | Power input to the switching stage                 |
| 10, 11, 16, 17, 18, 25 | LX | Switch node, goes to the inductor          |
| 12 to 15, 19 | PGND | Power ground                                       |
| 20  | BOOT  | Bootstrap cap to LX                                         |
| 21  | VCC   | Internal 5V LDO output. Proves the chip is alive            |
| 23  | SS    | Soft-start capacitor                                        |

## Diagnosis, powered

Take these in order. Black probe on ground.

| Reading           | Expect  | If wrong                                              |
|-------------------|---------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| Pin 7 AIN         | 12V     | Feed to the regulator is broken. Follow it back.      |
| Pin 21 VCC        | ~5V     | AIN present but no VCC: chip is dead. Replace.        |
| Pin 2 EN          | > 1.6V  | Low: something upstream is holding it off. See below. |
| Inductor (LX side)| rail V  | AIN, VCC and EN all good but 0V out: chip is dead.    |

Pin 2 is next to the pin 1 dot, which makes it easy to find. Pins 7 and 21
are fine pitch, so probe the nearest capacitor instead where possible:
the input cap sits on AIN/VIN, and there is normally a small cap on VCC.

## EN on 3090 boards

One repair thread on a 3090 with missing 1.8V found the GS9216 EN pin fed
from a pair of AND gates elsewhere on the board, with both gate inputs at
3.3V on a working card. So EN low does not necessarily mean the chip is at
fault; trace where EN comes from and check the inputs of whatever drives it.
